My Name is \_\_\_\_.
====================

"Hey, angel. Could you move a bit? Your wings are blocking my lunchbox." I shift to my left, hoping to provide more room. I adjust the speed as we hit an 60 mph zone. The train slows down, and I lean back in my seat,

"Is this good?" I ask.

"Yeah, that's better. I can reach it now." I turn to face her. Mint. My superior, here more so to keep me in check than to provide company.

"It's not nearly lunchtime yet... I don't think you should eat it so early." She disregards me, eagerly opening her lunchbox like a schoolchild. Despite her supposedly keeping me in check, sometimes I feel like a chastising mother. I can see the contents of fried pork over white rice with green onions. Mints chestnut brown hair shifts with her movements as she greedily shoves a piece of fried pork into her mouth. Practically melting at the flavor.

"Oh, it's fine! It'll be just fine!" she says, grinning. It's not fine. She will be hungry again in an hour. She's insufferable when she's hungry. She suddenly turns to me, shoving a piece of fried pork in my face. "It's so good! Try a piece! I made it at home!"

I lean back, my wings folding closer to my body. "I don't like eating meat," I say, and she continues to hold it out in front of me, looking dejected. We leave the 80mph zone, and I glance out the window, before turning back to her, and she slumps forward in defeat, before eating it herself.

"You angels are so prudent.." she sighs, before the lights flicker off as the emergency brakes cut on, shoving both of us forward. "Guh- Oi, angel! What's happening?! An Attack?!" The contrast from earlier is jarring. I wonder if her kindness is just an act to make me more agreeable.

"I have a name, you know," I murmur. Sighing. "But I'm not sure yet... could just be a malfunction." The train has fully stopped now. I stand up, walking over to the door connecting the engineer's room to the passenger cars. "I'll go reassure our passengers. Angel attack or not, it's still protocol,"

I move to unlock the door, before stepping through into the first passenger car. "Extremely sorry, but we seem to be having some technical issues. Please wait patiently so we can get up and running again." I look around and I'm hit with a barrage of questions.

"Is this an angel attack?!"

"How long will we be waiting?"

I simply nod. "Thank you for your consideration." I say before moving onto the next car. Repeating the process over and over again. Once I finish, I make my way back up when the door to the car in front of me startles everyone.

It's mint. "You were taking a while. I was worried." She says, looking around the car. Most are murmuring. Parents trying to quiet crying children. The tension in the darkened car is high.

"H-Hey, do you hear that?" One woman speaks up and everyone goes quiet to listen, save the sniffling of the kids. Faintly, we do hear it.

  

"Warning. Angel descent! Everyone stay indoors! Warning. Angel descent!" That's when everyone freaks out. The kids grow louder. Mint tries to calm everyone down, but it's barely effective. One woman even prays. Ironic for the situation.

There's a thud on the roof, silencing everyone. The cabin creaks as wind blows past. The woman prays faster.

Mint unholsters her gun. It's some modern pistol. I wouldn't know its name. I summon my own weapon.

  

a Gewehr 1998.

  

Only angelic weapons, or guns with angelic rounds, can harm angels like me.

An explosion tears through the roof of the train, shattering the windows. Everyone screams.

I aim my rifle at the hole in the ceiling, just as the angel drops down, her own rifle trained on me. A bayonet is fixed to the end. Everyone freezes. Some passengers begin to cry.

"Fear not humans, you will be returned to your heavenly father. You should be grateful." I need to get this off the train… I'm not sure if there are more angels outside, either. The opposing angels' eyes flick to my halo.

"Traitor." She snaps, charging, bayonet aimed at my throat. I shift to the right, and the blade barely whizzes past my face. It lodges itself into the man sitting behind me. He slumps forward.

Everyone screams, running to the exits on either side of the train.

Mint aims, shooting twice. The angel reacts quickly, and the bullets deflect off her wings before returning fire. Mint immediately drops to the ground to avoid the shot.

"Angel, help!" Mint yells, sliding across the floor to avoid another shot that almost turned her blood into a new coat of paint for the walls.

The attacking angel falters, confused. Why would someone ask for help from the thing trying to kill them?

"I have a name!" I slide the luminescent bullets into the gun, closing the bolt with a click before taking aim. The attacking angel dives out of the way. I shoot. It misses.

Mint takes advantage of the moment, slamming the attacking angel with a kick to the waist. The angel is stuck halfway against the seat, flailing wildly. Mint fires. I do too. I empty the five shots in my rifle before reloading smoothly. Mint lets off fifteen shots in less than three seconds. The hammer of her gun slamming back and snapping forward rapidly. I shoot. She shoots. Blood sprays with each shot.

I stop. Then Mint stops. The angel lie there, wings bent and broken, riddled with holes. It was dead. Like, really dead. 

  

A few moments pass, and the lights flicker back on. The soundscape of the train returns, hums and buzzes and clicks. Mint reaches into her uniform pocket, pulling out a hand-radio.

"Mint of company two speaking. Angel neutralization confirmed, on route C, block G-F. One confirmed civilian casualty." She takes a breath. "Requesting a cleanup crew as soon as possible. Out." She clicks it off.

I slump into a seat that isn't covered in viscera, and mint follows suit. We both sit for a few minutes before I stand. Mint looks up, about to do the same. "I'll be right back." I rub my lip with my thumb. "I'm getting my lunch." Mint grins, sitting back down.

"You hypocrite!" she laughs, leaning back and closing her eyes. "You really saved my skin back there." I stop walking. "Thanks." She says. I stare at her.

"Yeah. of course." I open the door, stepping into the next car before closing it behind me.

The silence of the empty car envelops me, and I stand there, resting in the peace. Until I remember the angel. With her now broken and bent backwards wings; riddled with holes, covered in blood. Her sightless eyes.

My hand flies to my mouth, but not fast enough. I retch and stagger. When I'm done heaving, I flick my hand away. "Disgusting…" I mutter.

  

"Whose side are you on?" That's what my master, Dusty, asked me when I was training to be a civil servant that also specializes in killing my own kind: Angels. I was confused. "What?" I asked. It had been an absurd question. Of course I'm on the humans' side. Had I not proven that by just being there?

  

"Whose side are you on?" She repeats, moving her black hair from in front of her face. "In this war, where humanity is being punished for our collective sins, whose side are you on? Angels, or humans," It's a question I could answer easily.

"Humans." I look away. "I like what's come of humanity. They have good food…"

Dusty laughs. The wind blows through, and my wings shift closer to my body. She pulls a pack of cigarettes from her pocket, then a lighter. She takes out a stick of tobacco, placing it in between her lips. She cups it to block the wind.

Flick. Flick.

Flick.

The flame dances from the lighter, illuminating her face. The cigarette catches, and she slides the lighter back into her pocket while taking a long drag.

"Sucky answer," she responds, slowly blowing out billows of smoke. "Belief in human culture won't get you where you want." She holds up a finger. "You have no real stakes in our survival. What you need is faith. Built through both trials and tribulations." She smiles.

"Schooling me on faith is a bit.." I pause. "tone deaf, don't you think?" I eye her cautiously. I'm not sure what she's planning, but I don't like it.

"Survive however long I feel like." She stretches, unholstering her gun. Fully loaded with angelic rounds, I'm sure. "And I'll have you temporarily assigned to the civil servant sector while I figure out a place for you with some combat." She raises the pistol and I flinch. "Fail and you're dead." I shift my weight.
