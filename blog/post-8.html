RINGU

October 18, 2026

I recently watched Ringu（リング）and it was so good!
I loved the sound design and music in the movie, it really hammered home the impending dread, especially when watching the tape.

One thing I appreciated that this movie had done was not show Sadako too much. It was terrifying seeing her grainy figure on the screen, because she was extremely obscured!
Also when Takayama was writing and Sadako stood infront of him, and he absolutely could not look up for fear of what'd happen, was really tense!

All in all this movie was a 10/10! I'll definetly be getting a bluray soon!
