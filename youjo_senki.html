幼女ようじょ戦記せんき

Saga Of Tanya The Evil

Youjo Senki (Saga Of Tanya The Evil) is a military isekai anime
based on a web-novel by Carlo Zen,
following a salaryman turned child soldier Tanya Von Degurechaff.
Tanya has two goals: Beat God in a bet, and live a peaceful life.
